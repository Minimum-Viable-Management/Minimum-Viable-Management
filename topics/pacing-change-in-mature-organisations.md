# 🎚️ Pacing Change in Mature Organisations

Change sticks when leaders pick the right battles, pace their bets, and protect the stability that engineers rely on.

## 🔑 Key Points

- You cannot change everything at once, so sequence the battles that matter and build credibility through delivery.
- Match the pace of change to the mission, preserving deliberate practices where accuracy and trust are critical.
- When you destabilise a norm, offer a new stability anchor so people can keep doing good work.
- Small, reversible experiments reveal which constraints are real and which are inherited habits.
- Leaders absorb the first risk of a change, taking responsibility if the trial fails.

## 🛠️ Put It Into Practice

1. Publish a six-month change map that names one or two norms you will challenge and what will stay stable.
2. Run a two-week test for a process change with explicit success and rollback criteria.
3. Name the stability substitute you will offer before changing a core workflow.
4. After each experiment, share what you learned and either make it permanent or revert.
